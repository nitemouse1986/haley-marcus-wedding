Good to know

Questions, answered

What should I wear?

Think elegant autumn: cocktail or formal, with a wrap or jacket for the evening. The ceremony may be on stone or grass, so block heels and flats are kinder than stilettos. Soft earth tones such as olive, sand and terracotta will look lovely in the Istrian light. Final dress code to be confirmed.

Do I need a passport or visa?

Everyone needs a valid passport. Croatia is in the EU and the Schengen Area, and entry rules depend on your citizenship. Please check the official requirements for your country well before you book.

What currency do they use?

Croatia uses the euro. Cards are widely accepted, and small cash is handy for cafes, taxis and tips.

What will the weather be like?

Mid-October in Istria is usually mild, with pleasant afternoons, cooler evenings in the hills and a chance of rain. Bring a jacket, a light layer and a small umbrella.

Can I bring a plus-one or children?

Your invitation names everyone we have saved a seat for. Use the RSVP form to tell us how many are in your party, and write to us if you have questions about little ones.

What language is spoken?

Croatian, with Italian widely spoken in Istria and plenty of English at the hotel. A cheerful hvala (thank you) and molim (please) will go a long way.
